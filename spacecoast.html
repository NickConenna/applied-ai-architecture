01

The industry is the geography

L3Harris, Lockheed, Northrop, Collins, Jacobs, Leidos, Embraer, and Astrion all run production floors on the Space Coast. SpaceX and Blue Origin fly hardware built by the same labor pool. No other U.S. county concentrates AI-plus-physical-systems work this tightly.

02

Built for a single operating base

Melbourne, Titusville, and Cape Canaveral sit inside a 25-mile radius — proximity between employer, launch range, and manufacturing floor that no other aerospace hub in the country compresses this tightly.

03

Thesis match, not just proximity

YSKAIPE's pivot into "planetary space suits" isn't a metaphor here — Collins Aerospace is under active NASA contract building the next lunar EVA suit two hours down the road.

Execution Cadence

One target, one day, in tier order. The goal isn't ten simultaneous pitches — it's ten real, specific conversations, sequenced so the easiest doors open first and build the case for the harder ones.

Access-point targets go first because a yes there compounds: an insider connection at Jacobs Technology or Astrion makes every later conversation on the Cape easier.

  • Day 4 Tier 2 — Collins Aerospace
