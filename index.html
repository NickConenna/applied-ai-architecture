    Questions owners ask.

    Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?

    No one can, and you should be wary of anyone who says otherwise. What we can do is strengthen what AI tools look at when they choose: recent reviews, accurate listings, and clear information about what you do and where. Then we check every month and show you where you stand. Google Maps still matters most for local searches, so the same work helps you there too.

    Will my customers know it’s AI?

    It introduces itself honestly as your business’s assistant. Most callers care that they got an answer and a time, not who typed it. If someone asks for a person, it takes a message and sends it to you right away.

    Do I need a new phone number?

    Usually not. In most setups, missed and after-hours calls forward from your existing number. We’ll confirm what works with your phone provider on the first call.

    What if it gets something wrong?

    It only answers from information you approve. Anything outside your rules goes to you with a summary instead of a guess. Every conversation is logged, so you can read exactly what was said.

    We’re a dental or medical practice. Is patient information handled properly?

    Setups that touch patient records need extra agreements and settings. We go through exactly what applies to your practice before anything goes live, and keep patient details out of the system until that’s in place.

    Is there a contract?

    No long contract. After setup it’s month to month, and your data stays yours if you cancel.

    What calendar and tools does it work with?

    Most common calendars and booking systems. Tell me what you use on the call and I’ll tell you straight whether it fits.
