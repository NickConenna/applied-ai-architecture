Why now.

The work AI can do sits in SG&A.

Documents, reconciliations, research, drafting, reporting, routine customer questions. AI now does this work in production, with people handling exceptions. That’s margin, not a demo.

Build once, roll across.

Most portfolio companies share the same back-office and reporting problems. A system proven in one company can be adapted for the next in a fraction of the time.

Few companies have it running.

Nearly every company has AI subscriptions. Very few have AI running the work that drives their numbers. That gap is the opportunity during your hold period.

A 2026 Goldman Sachs survey of 1,256 small businesses found only 14% had fully integrated AI into core operations.

The value is in execution: who can actually build it inside a company with a management team, a P&L, and a hold period.

Questions sponsors and CEOs ask.

How do you work with management teams that are already stretched?

The diagnostic takes a few hours of management time across one week. During a build we need one owner on the company side for about an hour a week, plus access to the people who do the work today.

Can this run across several portfolio companies?

Yes, and that’s where the economics improve. Start with one company, prove the number, then adapt the system for the next. The Portfolio AI partner engagement is built for that.

Why a small firm instead of a large consultancy?

Speed and accountability. You work directly with the person building the system, decisions happen in hours, and you pay for building rather than layers of staffing. If a project genuinely needs a larger team, we’ll say so before it starts.

How do you handle confidential and deal data?

NDA and data room terms first. We use business-grade AI accounts that don’t train on customer data, keep data in systems the company or sponsor owns, and agree in writing what can be sent to a model.

Do you replace the company’s IT team or vendors?

No. We build alongside them and inside their systems where possible, and hand everything over documented.

We’re not PE-backed. Is this for us?

Yes. Everything here works the same for an owner-led company: a one-week diagnostic, a baseline, then a build that pays for itself. Engagements are billed directly to the company.

Is this only for Charlotte firms?

No. We’re based in the Charlotte area and glad to meet in person, but most of the work is remote and we work with sponsors and companies anywhere in the US.
