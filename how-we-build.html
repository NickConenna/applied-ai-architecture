How we make sure it’s right before it ships.

Fast doesn’t mean careless. Before anything goes live, it has to pass a test built from your own work.

  1. You show us the real job

    On a short call, you walk us through the work the way your best person does it.

    “Here’s how we build a renewal proposal.”

  2. We load your real information

    Your price list, services, hours, and policies, plus the rules you’ve never written down.

    “We never discount below 15% without the CFO.”

  3. You approve the answer key

    We turn real examples into a test with the right result for each. You check it before we build anything.

    40 real cases, 40 approved results.

  4. It goes live only when it passes

    We run the test, fix what’s off, and run it again. It launches only after passing every case twice in a row.

    No passing score, no launch.
