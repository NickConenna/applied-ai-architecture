Section 03 — How I work

Context × Loops

Two inputs decide the quality of anything I build with a model. Everything else is tooling preference.

Frame

State the job in the operator's words, not the system's. If I can't, I don't understand it yet.

Load

Give the model the real context — the constraint, the history, the thing everyone in the room already knows.

Verify

Build the check first. Ground truth, a test, a person who'd notice. No verifier, no loop.

Correct

Run it again with what failed. Short cycles, visible each time. Stop when it holds twice.

Most of what I've published argues one point: the framework you pick is a detail, and the verifier you build is the whole thing.
