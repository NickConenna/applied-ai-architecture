The pressure on margin.

  • Quotes built by hand from RFQs, drawings, and price lists
  • Purchase orders arriving by email and PDF, keyed into the ERP manually
  • Supplier price changes not caught before they hit margin
  • Inside sales and customer service fielding the same order-status questions
  • Experienced estimators and inside sales hard to replace
  • Pricing inconsistent across reps and branches

Where we’d start.

Example plays, not client results. The diagnostic ranks which of these, or others, pays most for a specific company.

Quote generation

What AI does
Reads RFQs and specs, matches items to your catalog and price rules, and drafts the quote for an estimator to approve.
What changes
Faster quote turnaround, more quotes per estimator, consistent pricing.
Typical effort
Three to six weeks

Order entry from email

What AI does
Extracts line items from emailed and PDF purchase orders and stages them in the ERP for review.
What changes
Fewer keying errors, inside sales freed for selling.
Typical effort
Two to four weeks

Supplier spend and price variance

What AI does
Compares supplier invoices with agreed pricing and flags variances and consolidation opportunities.
What changes
Margin protected before price increases flow through.
Typical effort
Two to four weeks

Order-status and product answers

What AI does
Answers customer questions on order status, lead times, and specifications from your ERP and product data, routing exceptions to staff.
What changes
Faster answers, fewer interruptions for the team.
Typical effort
Three to five weeks
