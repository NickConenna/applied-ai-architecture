Everything we offer, and how each one works.

CoreKit AI is agentic first. AI does the heavy lifting, around the clock, on everything you share. People step in where judgment, taste, or hands matter: me, and vetted partner contractors when a job needs them.

How it works

Every offer runs on the same loop. Each one makes the next one smarter.

  1. 1. StartBuy the Core Kit, then add anything you like, now or later, from your member area.
  2. 2. Tell us onceYour answers, numbers, and briefs go into one customer file. Anything you've already shared pre-fills the next form.
  3. 3. Agents workAI drafts, models, researches, and builds from your whole file, not one form at a time.
  4. 4. People step inI review everything that leaves, make the judgment calls, and bring in a vetted partner when a job needs hands: design, development, photography, or a trade.
  5. 5. Delivered, and compoundingWork lands in your files with a status you can watch. Your file grows, and your AI partner gets sharper with every step.
AgentsYour AI partner, baseline, customer file, drafts, models, first builds. Fast, always on, and grounded in your own data.
NickReview, judgment, taste, and accountability. Notes within one business day; briefs answered within two.
PartnersVetted contractors for work that needs people: specialist design, extra development hands, photography, local trades. You know who and why before they start.
